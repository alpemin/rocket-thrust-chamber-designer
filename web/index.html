Equations and assumptions

Ideal rocket: c* = √(γRTc) / (γ√((2/(γ+1))^((γ+1)/(γ−1)))); exit Mach from the area–Mach relation; CF includes the pressure-thrust term ε(pe−pa)/pc. Throat area At = F / (CFpc). Chamber volume from characteristic length L*. Convergent: 30° cone with a 1.5 Rt throat arc. Divergent: Rao 80% bell as a quadratic Bézier from a 0.382 Rt arc. Heat transfer: Bartz with σ-correction, recovery factor Pr1/3. Liner thermal stress: σ = Eαqt / (2(1−ν)k). The structural jacket is Inconel 718, sized by hoop stress with coolant pressure assumed at 1.5 pc.


Combustion properties are approximate, single-point values at typical mixture ratios (frozen, no dissociation shift); real designs use NASA CEA. Material properties are rough elevated-temperature values. Performance is ideal: real engines lose about 2–5% Isp to combustion efficiency and divergence. Use this for preliminary sizing, not for building hardware.
